The room let SGA fall to #5 (Ant #1, Wemby #2, AD #3, Jokić #4). The

threshold (1.5) — and the owner took Luka.

- **The standout follow was the whole game (+3.3pp).** The largest
  single-pick price cost yet measured, and the card flagged it in
  advance at the maximum volume the system has. SGA's elite FT%/ST
  shape was also the exact anchor this roster ended up missing.
- **Tail deviations were a wash (−0.5pp, noise).** Unlike m19's deep
  reaches (board 35/41), m20's tail picks sat ≤0.7 off the card — the
  price rule's cost concentrates where composite gaps are large, i.e.
  the top of the draft. Passing scarcity-shelf WCJ at #140 was
  defensible with the C floor already met (WCJ still vanished 2 picks
  later — the chip was right).
- **Interior structural add positive AGAIN (4-for-4: m13/m18/m19/m20).**
  Duren was the pinned TARGET at #68 ("Best C — shelf: 0 in window");
  removing him costs −1.9pp and a playoff-odds cliff (62% → 51%).

**Shape lesson (P2 class):** kept-total ranked 2nd in the room for the
second straight mock, but no category finished better than 4th — and
the finish (6th) tracked the missing locks, not the z-total. The room's
winner was a Wemby points-chaser at 39.3% with a *negative* kept-total
(−1.81): weekly H2H pays dominated categories, not breadth. m19's FT%
lock (rank 1) is what m20 never had — and it's what SGA at #5 would
have anchored.
