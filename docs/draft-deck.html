      Logic. The board, punt math, feed parsing, and council ruleset are ports of scripts/hoops.py / arena/arena.py, verified against engine output at build time; z-scores standardize over the draftable top-156 (replacement-anchored, codified 2026-07-30). Every layer beyond the composite ranking is display-only and causally tested: Strengths/Weaknesses are your live category ranks vs the field (🔒 = dominated); the ⏱ BUY NOW / TOSS-UP markers flag players unlikely to survive to your next pick under a room-mix survival model — value-drafting seats blended with market seats, recalibrated 2026-08-03 after the market-only model marked 24/24 doomed players quiet in mock 30 (no marker = calibrated safe-to-wait: 96% measured survival; September ADP sharpens the market half); the single 🎯 marks the system pick — the top blend50 score (50% marginal weekly-cats-won vs the room + 50% balanced value, deliberately punt-blind; per-player punt numbers ride the blue chips), or an urgent structural TARGET when a needed family's shelf is nearly empty; a TARGET label always reflects a roster need, while the same read renders as BOARD LEAN when it only describes what the board is offering (provenance split 2026-08-03, owner-reported); the same read also pins a sixth row under the Top-5 when its family made none of them — LAST CALL when urgent (the 🎯 moves onto it: take it over the Top-5) or DEPTH WATCH when informational (the 🎯 above stays the pick), named 2026-08-10 on owner request. The build-coherence strip checks your declared targets against every single-swap alternative on the roster as drafted (ON TARGET / OFF TARGET / WRONG TARGETS; display + declaration only; added 2026-08-05 after mock 32 punted steals over a rank-3-steals roster).
