      Logic. The board, punt math, feed parsing, and council ruleset are ports of scripts/hoops.py / arena/arena.py, verified against engine output at build time; z-scores standardize over the draftable top-156 (replacement-anchored, codified 2026-07-30). Every layer beyond the composite ranking is display-only and causally tested: Strengths/Weaknesses are your live category ranks vs the field (🔒 = dominated); the ⏱ BUY NOW / TOSS-UP markers flag players unlikely to survive to your next pick under a room-mix survival model — value-drafting seats blended with market seats, recalibrated 2026-08-03 after the market-only model marked 24/24 doomed players quiet in mock 30 (no marker = calibrated safe-to-wait: 96% measured survival; September ADP sharpens the market half); the single 🎯 marks the system pick — the top blend50 score (50% marginal weekly-cats-won vs the room + 50% balanced value, deliberately punt-blind; per-player punt numbers ride the blue chips), or an urgent structural TARGET when a needed family's shelf is nearly empty; a TARGET label always reflects a roster need, while the same read renders as BOARD LEAN when it only describes what the board is offering (provenance split 2026-08-03, owner-reported); the same read also pins a sixth row under the Top-5 when its family made none of them — LAST CALL when urgent (the 🎯 moves onto it: take it over the Top-5) or DEPTH WATCH when informational (the 🎯 above stays the pick), named 2026-08-10 on owner request. The build-coherence strip checks your declared targets against every single-swap alternative on the roster as drafted (ON TARGET / OFF TARGET / WRONG TARGETS; display + declaration only; added 2026-08-05 after mock 32 punted steals over a rank-3-steals roster). The style/archetype layer runs internally (TARGET, punt analyzer) and never prescribes the punt box, because punt-declaring measured −5 to −11pp on the original instrument (−3.1 to −6.4pp re-measured on the corrected bench weight, 2026-08-10 — direction survives at every calibration). Mock opponents are the 11 named league-mates (three-season behavioral profiles, E18); the synthetic market carries only ~0.45× the real board's divergence, so expect REAL rooms to produce deeper star falls than mocks until October ADP replaces the proxy. The judgment layer (per-player adjustments, ~marked estimates) is re-authored with each republish — enforced by build gate 5, which refuses any build whose judgment date trails the pull — and never applied to the mock AIs.
