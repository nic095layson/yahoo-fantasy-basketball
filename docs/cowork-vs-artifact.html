The Artifact

A self-contained page on a stable URL. No accounts, no runtime, no waiting — the entire player pool and the ranking math shipped to the browser. This page is one.

The call

This isn't a trade decision. It's a frontcourt pairing.

Keep the Cowork system as the sole source of truth. Build artifacts as read-only surfaces generated from it. The engine owns everything that requires compute, fresh data, state, or judgment — drafts, trades, the freshness rule, the confidence calls. An artifact owns everything that benefits from instant interaction, visual density, and a URL you can open on your phone or hand to a league mate.

The two failure modes to avoid: an artifact that pretends to be the engine (it will silently rot — the exact staleness problem your daily-refresh rule exists to kill), and a Cowork session doing work a page does better (re-running rank --punt six times to explore builds is a conversation; below, it's six clicks in four seconds).

Decision guide

Who gets the ball

Draft night
Engine, exclusively
The speed rule, snake attribution, the state handshake, confidence cards. Nothing else touches this. An artifact's only role is the morning after — rendering the finished league.
"Should I make this trade?"
Engine decides · artifact explains
The verdict needs fresh data and judgment — that's a Cowork question. But per-category deltas are exactly the kind of thing a visual page communicates better than a text table, especially if you want to show the other manager.
Exploring punt builds
Artifact
This is browsing, not deciding. Twenty rank --punt variations is a tedious conversation; below it's a toy you can play with until the shape of the build is obvious. Bring the finalists back to Cowork for the actual call.
Showing your league mates
Artifact, by definition
They don't have Claude and they don't want your terminal. A URL is the only format that travels.
Season-long monitoring
Hybrid — the pattern that matters
A scheduled Cowork session refreshes the CSV under the freshness rule, regenerates the page, republishes to the same URL. The engine stays the brain; the artifact becomes its always-current face. This is the pattern every build below uses.

    Honest limits

    What this page cannot do

    • It's frozen. Built July 22, 2026 from that day's pool. Giannis is on MIA here because the CSV says so — and if he's traded tomorrow, this page won't know until a session rebuilds it. The build date in the footer is load-bearing.
    • No judgment. It ranks; it doesn't recommend. No confidence percentages, no healthy-tiebreak, no punt-coherence warnings, no "why."
    • No draft state. It can't log a pick, and it never should — that's the engine's contract with draft_state.json.
    • Simplified matching. Search here is substring-only. The engine's nickname table, typo fuzzing, and surname-collision halts stay in Python where they belong.
    • Sandboxed on purpose. A strict content-security policy blocks every external request — no live NBA data, no CDNs, no analytics. What's shipped is all there is. (Available headroom on your account: pages can offer file downloads, and can call your claude.ai connectors with per-viewer consent — GitHub, Gmail, Google Drive, and Spotify are connected on the account; this page deliberately declares no such capability.)

    Four builds, in the order I'd draft them

    All four follow the hybrid pattern: Cowork generates, the artifact displays, redeploys keep the URL. None of them move state or judgment out of the engine.

    Pick 1

    Trade Court

    Pick the send side and the get side from the pool; per-category deltas, net value, and punt-aware verdicts render instantly — the visual version of hoops.py trade. Shareable with the other manager, which is half the point of a trade argument.

    Same data this page already embeds · pure client-side · buildable today

    Pick 2

    Draft Command Deck

    The morning-after league explorer: reads a finished draft_state.json, renders every roster, the category matrix as a heat grid, and head-to-head cards versus each opponent. The visual layer draft matrix and draft vs are begging for.

    Regenerated once, post-draft · zero draft-night involvement by design

    Pick 3

    Arena Film Room

    Championship% by strategy and by slot from arena/results, drift across generations, slot-intel visualized. Turns tournament JSON you currently read as text into something you can actually study.

    Rebuilt after each tournament run · reads results only, never touches the engine

    Pick 4

    Season Scoreboard

    Weekly matchup dashboard: your category standings, streaming targets, schedule density. The honest caveat: with the Yahoo API scrapped, inputs are pasted rosters or a scheduled session doing the research — this one is only as good as its refresh routine.
